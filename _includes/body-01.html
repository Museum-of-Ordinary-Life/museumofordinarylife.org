Why ordinary life?

The present is full of things history will wish someone had bothered to document.

01

The object

The receipt, key, charger, badge, bottle, bag, cable or handwritten note. The physical evidence of ordinary life.

02

The context

Where the thing lived, how it was used, what surrounded it, and the systems that made it meaningful.

03

The voice

“I use this every day.” “I hate this thing.” “I don’t know why I still have it.” Ordinary testimony is part of the collection.

One museum / Many local forms

Ordinary life is everywhere. Its stories should be, too.

The Museum of Ordinary Life is one museum and potentially many: a network capable of growing wherever people decide something ordinary deserves to be remembered.
